# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages. Use Bootstrap 5 selectively for responsive layout and components; no React migration.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers assessing candidates for AI/ML and software engineering roles.

## Product Purpose

Present Sanskriti Shevgaonkar's experience, projects, and technical writing so visitors can assess her work and initiate an interview or contact.

## Positioning

End-to-end applied AI/ML work that combines retrieval and model development, production engineering, and a security-aware mindset.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a single-page portfolio on desktop or mobile, review projects and experience, follow CV, GitHub, LinkedIn, or blog links, and contact Sanskriti.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site presents projects, experience, skills, education, writing, and contact information.
- GitHub Pages is the deployment target; keep the site statically hostable.
- Preserve responsive behavior, keyboard-accessible navigation and focus, and reduced-motion support.
- Use Bootstrap 5 selectively; the product remains plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Keep claims aligned with the supplied CV and existing project evidence. Do not invent metrics, clients, or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Sanskriti Shevgaonkar.
- The user wants a visually rich portfolio, relevant real project and blog imagery, and a distinctive presentation for the dissertation.
- No palette, typography, or visual era is mandated.

## Evidence on Hand

- CV-backed experience and project information in the existing portfolio.
- Project links, including the 3D virtual try-on dissertation and image-splicing/deepfake detection work.
- Local article imagery for the robotic-assisted surgery, neural network use-case, and Domino's predictive-ordering posts (`images/article-1.jpg` through `images/article-3.jpg`).
- Local project imagery for deepfake detection and virtual try-on (`images/portfolio-1.jpg` and `images/portfolio-2.jpg`); preserve any existing image credits.

## Product Principles

- Make relevant evidence easy for recruiters to assess.
- Prefer concrete work and verified claims over broad promises.
- Present applied AI/ML as an end-to-end engineering practice.
- Keep the experience accessible and usable across screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain responsive layouts, keyboard-operable navigation with visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
